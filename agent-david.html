David DiLisio

A Lifetime of Real Estate and Business Experience.

David DiLisio brings a lifetime of passion and enthusiasm for real estate and business to his brokerage work. He has many years of experience in property investment, property development, and business ownership.

David has successfully owned and operated multiple businesses, including a bottled-water company he operated for 25 years and a family fun center that he built and still owns. In addition to his business experience, he has developed several properties and has owned and renovated investment properties.

He is a member of the National Association of Realtors, the Massachusetts Association of Realtors, and the North Shore Association of Realtors.

My Commitment to You

Whether you are purchasing or selling a home, David is committed to using his real estate skills, experience, and market knowledge to create a great experience. He strives to provide professional, responsive, and friendly service that begins by carefully listening to and understanding clients' real estate needs and goals.

If you are looking for a competent and caring real estate professional to represent you in the marketplace, David welcomes the opportunity to talk.

Personal Background

David is a graduate of Fordham University's College of Business Administration, with a dual major in Marketing and Management. He has a love of horses and has trained and shown American Quarter Horses, competing at local, state, and national levels.

David also holds a third-degree black belt and is an avid gym enthusiast. A member of Kernwood Country Club, he pursues his passion for golf. David enjoys walks in the woods and on the beach with his wife, Karen, their son, Luke, and their collie, Brady.
