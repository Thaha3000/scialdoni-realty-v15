BROOKVIEW HEIGHTS · METHUEN, MA

Designed for Life on the North Shore.

Contact agent for details ↗

Brookview Heights is described in the project materials as a planned 28-lot single-family community off Old Ferry Drive in Methuen, with conservation land bordering the homesites. Lot 1 is identified as outside this development. Five preliminary home designs illustrate options with first-floor primary suites, four bedrooms, two-car garages and approximately 2,400–2,775 square feet of finished living area. Initial materials target the first three homes for Spring 2027. Construction is attributed to M&M Construction & Development LLC; please contact the agent or builder for current lot selection, pricing, designs, availability and construction updates.
