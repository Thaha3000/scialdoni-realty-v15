Mortgage Pre-Approval

A lender will review your income, monthly debts, credit history, and other financial information to determine the approximate loan amount for which you may qualify. Once that review is complete, the lender can provide you with a pre-approval letter.

Pre-approval can help you:

  • Know your purchasing power and focus your search.
  • Be prepared to act when the right home becomes available.
  • Strengthen your offer by demonstrating your financing preparation.
  • Shop with confidence within your expected budget.

What You May Need for Your Mortgage Application

Your lender will typically request financial and personal documentation, which may include:

  1. Personal information — current address and employment details.
  2. Credit authorization — permission to review your credit.
  3. Income documentation — recent tax returns and W-2 forms.
  4. Bank statements — recent account statements.
  5. Proof of current income — pay stubs or other income documentation.
  6. Outstanding debts — information on loans and other financial obligations.

Exact requirements depend on your lender and loan program.
