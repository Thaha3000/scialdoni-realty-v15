Edward Scialdoni

Experience. Integrity. Results.

With more than 30 years of entrepreneurial and business experience, Ed Scialdoni brings a unique combination of real estate knowledge, business expertise, negotiation skills, and personal commitment to every client he serves.

A U.S. Marine Corps veteran and Bentley University graduate, Ed began his professional career building successful businesses in the transportation and food industries. His longtime passion for real estate investing eventually led him to make real estate his full-time profession in 2008. Today, he brings that entrepreneurial mindset to Scialdoni Realty, providing clients with experienced, personalized representation throughout every step of the buying and selling process.

Real Estate Experience That Goes Beyond the Transaction

Ed's experience extends well beyond traditional residential brokerage. As a principal in Brookview Heights LLC, Ed is involved in a 28-lot residential subdivision in Methuen, Massachusetts. This hands-on experience gives him an understanding of real estate from a perspective few agents possess—from land development and construction to financing, valuation, marketing, and the many decisions involved in bringing a residential project from concept to completion.

Ed's previous experience as a loan originator also gives his clients valuable insight into the financing side of a transaction. Combined with decades of business and negotiation experience, he understands that a successful real estate transaction isn't simply about finding a property or accepting an offer. It's about understanding the entire picture and making informed decisions.

A Client-First Approach

At Scialdoni Realty, Ed believes that successful real estate representation starts with listening. Every client has different goals, circumstances, priorities, and expectations. Ed takes the time to understand those needs and then develops a strategy around them.

For Buyers

Experienced guidance in finding the right property, evaluating value, structuring an offer, negotiating terms, and navigating the transaction from offer to closing.

For Sellers

Strategic pricing, thoughtful preparation, professional presentation, comprehensive marketing, strong negotiation, and hands-on management throughout the entire selling process.

Your goals come first. Your best interests guide the strategy.

Experience You Can Put to Work

Over the years, Ed has developed the ability to look at a real estate transaction from multiple perspectives—buyer, seller, investor, developer, business owner, and financing professional. That broader perspective can make a meaningful difference when evaluating opportunities, anticipating potential challenges, negotiating terms, or making important decisions.

Ed believes clients deserve more than someone who simply opens doors or puts a property on the MLS. They deserve an experienced professional who is accessible, responsive, straightforward, and personally involved from beginning to end.

Beyond Real Estate

Ed is a longtime North Shore resident and has been married to his wife, Patti, for more than 40 years. Together, they are proud parents of Philip and Laura and grandparents to three grandchildren, including Victoria and twin boys William and Patrick.

Giving back to the community has always been important to Ed. He is a former treasurer of Salem Youth Soccer and has served as a high school soccer referee for more than 20 years. He continues to stay involved in youth soccer as a coach in Beverly, where he enjoys coaching and spending time with his grandchildren.

Ed was also a longtime ski professional at Sunday River. When he's not working with clients, he enjoys staying active, spending time on the golf course at Kernwood Country Club, and participating in organizations including the Christopher Columbus Society and the AOH.

Putting Your Needs First Every Step of the Way

Whether you're buying your first home, selling a longtime family property, investing in real estate, or considering your next move, Ed brings experience, perspective, and personal attention to the process.
